About Me

Welcome to my academic homepage. I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Mathematics at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology.

My mathematical path began at an early age: after completing the advanced matriculation examination in mathematics at age 14, I initiated my academic university studies through the Open University of Israel (Academia in High School program). Following a transition to independent homeschooling and completing advanced transfer track coursework at the Technion, I formally matriculated as an undergraduate student at the Technion, where I am pursuing my degree in pure mathematics.

Current Academic Focus

My mathematical interests revolve around pure and theoretical mathematics, with an emphasis on structural, theory-building perspectives in algebraic geometry, arithmetic geometry, and category theory. During the current academic semester (Winter 2027), I am engaging in advanced coursework spanning modern algebra, differentiable manifolds, real functions and measure theory, field theory, topology, and algebraic numbers, alongside dedicated exposure to departmental research.

Research & Interests

My mathematical interests center on algebraic and arithmetic geometry, and more broadly on giving geometric meaning to algebraic and arithmetic objects:

  • Algebraic & Arithmetic Geometry: Schemes, curves, moduli spaces, and vector bundles, with an emphasis on understanding algebraic structures through geometric intuition.
  • Local & Global Fields and the Three-Way Analogy: The parallel worlds of Riemann surfaces, function fields over finite fields, and number fields (\(\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})\))—studying each setting in its own right, as well as the deep structural dictionary connecting them.
  • Galois Theory & Galois Representations: The structure of the absolute Galois group \(\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})\), Galois representations, and their geometric interpretations.
  • The Langlands Program & Geometric Langlands: Moduli stacks of \(G\)-bundles (\(\mathrm{Bun}_G\)), sheaves, and the bridge between arithmetic and geometry.

Mathematical Perspective

When I study mathematics, I am less interested in isolated tricks or purely formal manipulations, and much more interested in the underlying meaning behind a definition or a theorem. I naturally gravitate toward Grothendieck's style of thinking: finding the right conceptual framework and geometric picture in which objects and their relationships become transparent, rather than forcing results through ad hoc computations.

For me, understanding a mathematical object means seeing what kind of space or geometry it represents, how it behaves locally and globally, and how it fits into a larger unified picture.
