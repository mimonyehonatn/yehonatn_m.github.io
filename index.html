About Me

Welcome to my academic homepage. I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Mathematics at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology.

My mathematical path began at an early age: after completing the advanced matriculation examination in mathematics at age 14, I initiated my academic university studies through the Open University of Israel (Academia in High School program). Following a transition to independent homeschooling and completing advanced transfer track coursework at the Technion, I formally matriculated as an undergraduate student at the Technion, where I am pursuing my degree in pure mathematics.

Current Academic Focus

My mathematical interests revolve around pure and theoretical mathematics, with an emphasis on structural, theory-building perspectives in algebraic geometry, arithmetic geometry, and category theory. During the current academic semester (Winter 2027), I am engaging in advanced coursework spanning modern algebra, differentiable manifolds, real functions and measure theory, field theory, topology, and algebraic numbers, alongside dedicated exposure to departmental research.

Future Academic Plans & Upcoming Projects

Looking ahead to Spring 2027, I plan to undertake an undergraduate research project under the supervision of Prof. Howard Nuer at the Technion, focusing on the moduli stack of vector bundles (\(\mathrm{Bun}_G\)) and foundational aspects of the Geometric Langlands program.

I plan to complete my B.Sc. degree in Mathematics at the Technion in Spring 2027, followed directly by M.Sc. studies in pure mathematics starting in Winter 2028, with the ultimate objective of pursuing doctoral research and an academic career in pure mathematics.

Methodology & Approach

I am drawn to top-down, conceptual mathematics that emphasizes large structural perspectives. I spend significant time working through foundational treatises independently—ranging from Serre's arithmetic texts to modern lecture notes on algebraic stacks and cluster algebras.

Research & Interests

My mathematical interests lie primarily in abstract algebra, arithmetic geometry, and category theory:

  • The Langlands Program & Geometric Langlands: Moduli stack of \(G\)-bundles (\(\mathrm{Bun}_G\)), automorphic sheaves, and categorical perspectives.
  • Arithmetic & Algebraic Geometry: Moduli spaces, vector bundles on curves, schemes, and Galois representations.
  • Representation Theory & Reflection Groups: Coxeter groups, root systems, and related combinatorial structures.
  • Cluster Algebras & Invariant Theory: Relations to Galois theory, fixed fields, and modular groups.
  • Number Theory: \(p\)-adic analysis, local fields, Hilbert symbols, and Fourier analysis on number fields.
